About

A family-run McDonald's operator

Schmitt Management is a privately held McDonald's franchisee. Our 12 locations serve communities including Naperville, Oswego, Aurora, and the surrounding western suburbs of Chicago. We are not a public-facing e-commerce business — this website serves as our corporate information page and the public-facing reference for our internal communications practices.

Legal entity: Schmitt Management

Industry: Quick-service restaurants (McDonald's franchise)

Locations: 12 restaurants, western Chicago suburbs

Corporate office: Aurora, IL area

Operations

Run by the people closest to the guest

Day-to-day operations are managed by general managers at each restaurant, overseen by area supervisors and the ownership team. Our corporate functions — payroll, accounting, IT, and people operations — are centralized at the corporate office.

To coordinate across 12 restaurants and 600+ employees, we operate an internal operations platform that handles task assignment, complaint tracking, manager dashboards, and internal communications. The platform is used only by our W-2 employees and managers; it is not accessible to customers or the general public.

SMS Communications Policy

How we use text messaging

What we send

Schmitt Management sends operational SMS text messages to our managers, supervisors, and corporate staff. These messages include:

  • Personalized links to internal manager dashboards
  • Task assignments, reminders, and status updates
  • Acknowledgements of customer complaints routed to the responsible manager
  • Scheduling notes and operational announcements
  • Period-end bonus and performance summaries

We do not send marketing, promotional, or consumer-facing text messages of any kind. We do not text customers. We do not share recipient phone numbers with third parties.

How recipients opt in

Recipients of our SMS communications are W-2 employees of Schmitt Management. During onboarding, each manager provides their mobile phone number on an employee information form and signs an acknowledgement that includes the following language:

"By providing my mobile number, I consent to receive operational text messages from Schmitt Management regarding my work duties, including links to manager dashboards, task notifications, scheduling notes, and operational reminders. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies based on operational activity. I can opt out at any time by replying STOP, or request help by replying HELP."

A signed copy of this acknowledgement is retained in each employee's human-resources file.

How to opt out

Any recipient may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message received from us. This will immediately end all SMS communications from Schmitt Management. To opt back in, reply START. For help or questions, reply HELP or email ops@schmittmcd.com.

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies based on operational activity, but is typically less than 10 messages per recipient per week.

Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

What we collect

For our internal operations platform, we collect: employee name, role, work email, mobile phone number, restaurant assignment, and work-related performance metrics. We do not collect personal financial information, government identifiers, health information, or any data unrelated to the employee's work with Schmitt Management.

How we use it

We use the information collected solely to operate our business: assigning tasks, routing communications, tracking restaurant-level performance, and meeting our obligations as a McDonald's franchisee. We do not use this information for marketing of any kind.

SMS data specifically

Mobile phone numbers collected for SMS communications are stored in our internal operations database and are used only to send the operational messages described above. We do not share, sell, rent, or otherwise disclose mobile phone numbers or SMS message content to any third party for marketing or any other purpose. Mobile data is processed by our SMS service provider (Salesmsg) solely to deliver the messages we send; Salesmsg's own privacy policy applies to that processing.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, excluding aggregators and providers of the Text Message services.

Data retention

Employee data is retained for the duration of employment plus the period required by applicable employment-records regulations. SMS message records are retained for operational and audit purposes.

Your rights

Employees may request a copy of the information we hold about them, or request correction of inaccurate information, by emailing ops@schmittmcd.com.
